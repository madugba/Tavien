**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]. Part of the Terms of Service. Applies to you, your members and your agents.

## 1. Principles
Use Tavieri lawfully, honestly and with respect for the people your agents and meetings affect. You are responsible for everything your account, agents and connected tools do.

## 2. Meetings, recording and people
You must not:
- Record, transcribe or add an AI to a conversation without authority to do so, without telling participants, or without any consent the law requires.
- Hide, rename, disable or work around the AI's disclosure, its "AI" label, or its consent notices, or deploy it as a covert listener.
- Keep capturing after a participant or host has asked you to stop or removed the AI.
- Use Tavieri to identify people by voice or face, build voiceprints or face templates, infer emotions or sensitive traits, or track individuals.
- Impersonate a real person with voice or avatar, create a deepfake of any real person, or present AI as human to deceive someone. Only the provided stock or synthetic avatars are allowed. [UPDATE if own-likeness is later offered with verified consent.]

## 4. Security and platform integrity
No attempt to: breach, probe or overload Tavieri; bypass policy, approval, budget, rate or tenancy controls; extract other users' data, system prompts or keys; use prompt injection or poisoned tool content against us, other users or third parties; run malware; or use the service to attack other systems. ## 5. Agents, tools and third parties
- Connect only accounts and tools you are authorised to use, and honour their terms (including automation, scraping and bot rules of other platforms).

- Keep approval policies appropriate for the risk. Do not auto-approve high-impact actions such as payments, deletions or external sends without safeguards.

## 6. Regulated and high-stakes uses
Do not use Tavieri as the sole basis for decisions with legal or similarly significant effect on people (employment, credit, insurance, housing, education, law enforcement, immigration, healthcare). Meaningful human review is required. Do not use it for emergency services or safety-critical control. Do not process special-category or children's data unless your organisation has the lawful basis and safeguards.

## 7. Model keys and resources
Use only API keys you are authorised to use. Do not resell the service or use it to build or train a competing model. Do not abuse free tiers or evade usage limits.
