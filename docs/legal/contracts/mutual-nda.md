# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Effective date:** [DATE]

**Parties:** [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [ADDRESS] ("**Tavieri**"), and [COUNTERPARTY NAME], [COUNTERPARTY ADDRESS]
("**Counterparty**"), each a "**Party**", a "**Discloser**" when disclosing and a "**Recipient**" when
receiving.

**Purpose:** to evaluate and discuss a possible design-partner pilot, customer interview, or business
relationship concerning the Tavieri AI teammate (the "**Purpose**").

1. **Confidential Information** is non-public information disclosed by or for the Discloser in any form
   in connection with the Purpose that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood as
   confidential, including product plans, pre-release features and demos, architecture, security
   information, pricing, customer and business data, and the existence and terms of the discussions.
   Interview recordings and notes are Confidential Information of the person who provided them.
2. **Exclusions.** Not confidential if it (a) is or becomes public without Recipient's breach; (b) was
   lawfully known to Recipient before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the
   Confidential Information; or (d) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.
3. **Obligations.** Recipient will (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose; (b) not disclose
   it except to employees, contractors, advisers and, for Tavieri, its engaged sub-processors who need
   to know and are bound by written duties at least as protective; (c) protect it with at least
   reasonable care and the care it uses for its own; (d) not copy it beyond what the Purpose needs; and
   (e) notify the Discloser promptly of any unauthorised use or disclosure.
4. **AI tools.** Recipient must not input Discloser's Confidential Information into any AI tool or
   service that retains it for, or uses it for, model training or is not covered by confidentiality
   terms at least as protective as this NDA. Processing through enterprise-grade AI services bound by
   zero-retention, no-training terms, and under the Recipient's control, is permitted.
5. **Compelled disclosure.** Recipient may disclose to the extent required by law or a court or authority,
   after giving the Discloser prompt notice (if lawful) and reasonable help to seek protection, and
   disclosing only what is required.
6. **Personal data.** The Parties do not intend to exchange personal data beyond business contact details
   and interview participation. Any personal data that is exchanged is handled lawfully and the Parties
   will sign a separate data processing or transfer agreement if processing for the Purpose goes beyond
   that. Recordings of interviews require consent of every participant.
7. **No licence; no obligation.** Confidential Information remains the Discloser's property; no licence or
   right is granted except to use it for the Purpose. Nothing obliges either Party to disclose
   information, to proceed with any transaction, or to buy or sell anything. Feedback is dealt with in any
   later agreement (such as `design-partner-agreement.md`), not here. Confidential Information is provided
   "as is" without warranty.
8. **Term and return.** This NDA covers disclosures made for [2] years from the Effective date and
   obligations continue for [3] years after the end of that period, and for trade secrets as long as they
   qualify as such. On request or when the Purpose ends, Recipient returns or deletes Confidential
   Information (backups and legally required records excepted, and remaining subject to this NDA) and
   confirms in writing.
9. **Remedies.** Unauthorised use may cause irreparable harm; the Discloser may seek injunctive relief
   without proof of damages in addition to other remedies, as the applicable law allows.
10. **Whistleblower and legal carve-outs.** Nothing here prevents reporting suspected violations of law to
    authorities, or any disclosure protected by law. Under the US Defend Trade Secrets Act (18 U.S.C.
    section 1833(b)), an individual is not liable for confidential disclosure of a trade secret made in
    confidence to a government official or an attorney solely to report or investigate a suspected
    violation of law, or in a sealed court filing.
11. **General.** This is the entire agreement on its subject; changes only in writing signed by both
    Parties; no assignment without consent except to a successor of the business; electronic signatures
    and counterparts are valid; unenforceable terms are severed and the rest stands. Governing law and
    venue: [GOVERNING LAW / VENUE]. If a later agreement (for example a design partner agreement)
    contains its own confidentiality terms, those govern the later disclosures but this NDA continues for
    earlier ones.
12. **Export.** Neither Party will export Confidential Information in breach of applicable law.
